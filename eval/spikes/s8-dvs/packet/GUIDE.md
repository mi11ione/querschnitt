# Annotation guide (blind)
Task: for each parliamentary affair (one line: id | parliament | type | title | optional long title), decide whether it belongs on a list of "politische Geschäfte zum Thema Digitalisierung" as kept by Digital Administration Switzerland (DVS): affairs about the digital transformation of public administration and society — e-government, digital services, eID, registers and data, ICT infrastructure and ICT spending of the state, cybersecurity, digital voting, AI in administration, digital education infrastructure, data protection in digital contexts, digital platforms/media regulation. NOT digital: affairs that merely mention a website or e-mail in passing, or are about other topics.
Answer per line: `<id>\tY` or `<id>\tN` (Y = belongs on the list), plus `\t?` only if the title is genuinely undecidable. Judge from the given text only. Do not look anything up.
