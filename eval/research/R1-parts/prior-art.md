No model cards, no metrics. https://huggingface.co/kapllan (Kapllan = Veton Matoshi's HF handle [guess, he also authored rcds legal NLP datasets]).
  - **Training size**: header of `omikuji-train.txt` (de) = `15007 11515 233` → **15,007 German training docs, 11,515 TF-IDF features, 233 labels**. Hinge loss. https://huggingface.co/kapllan/omikuji-bonsai-parliament-spacy-de-all_topics-input_long/tree/main

Main topics: Staatspolitik, Geschichte, Parlament, Internationale Politik, Sicherheitspolitik, Europapolitik, Recht allgemein, Zivilrecht, Strafrecht, Gerichtswesen, Rechte und Freiheiten, Internationales Recht, Wirtschaft, Finanzwesen, Finanzplatz, Steuer, Soziale Fragen, Migration, Kultur, Sozialer Schutz, Gesundheit, Raumplanung und Wohnungswesen, Bildung, Medien und Kommunikation, Wissenschaft und Forschung, Beschäftigung und Arbeit, Verkehr, Umwelt, Tierwelt, Landwirtschaft, Energie. This is the **Parlamentsbibliothek / Curia Vista thematic scheme, NOT APS** [guess: matches parlament.ch "Sachbereich"/Themen tagging]. "Digitalisierung" is not a main topic; nearest sub-topics under "Medien und Kommunikation" (e.g. "internet und soziale medien"). https://huggingface.co/spaces/rcds/SwissParlTopicModelling/blob/main/topics_hierarchy.json

## 3. Cantonal / municipal prior work

### 3a. Canton Zürich KI-Pilot 2023 (Staatskanzlei + Statistisches Amt) — best-documented numbers in the field
- Task: route Kantonsrat Vorstösse (10–15/week) to Direktion → Amt → Abteilung. Two approaches: (1) supervised, (2) zero-shot semantic similarity vs the 129 responsibility blocks of Anhang 1 VOG RR. Human approves every suggestion; standalone browser app; first application of ZH "Leitlinien zum Einsatz KI in der Verwaltung". Press release 03.04.2023. https://www.zh.ch/de/news-uebersicht/mitteilungen/2023/politik-staat/statistik/ki-pilotprojekt.html
- Report V2.0 (25 p.): https://www.zh.ch/content/dam/zhweb/bilder-dokumente/organisation/direktion-der-justiz-und-des-innern/statistisches-amt/KI%20Pilot%20Bericht_V2.0.pdf
  - Supervised: German **DistilBERT and a GBERT-based** model, 80/20 split, Kantonsrat OGD affairs since 1987. **Direktion level test accuracy 92.5% (upsampled) / 93.44% (no upsampling)**; per-Direktion 87.3% (Bau) … 95.4% (Gesundheit), 98.4% Staatskanzlei (n=147, outlier). Amt level within JI: only 340 data points → **73%** (70% upsampled; Recht group 60.3%). All Ämter of 27 units: 90.36% but range 67–98.9%. Abteilungen: 93%.
  - **Zero-shot** (German pretrained NLI-style transformer, CPU only): 129 blocks → **estimated 73% accuracy at 241 s per affair**; collapsing to 35 or 8 blocks → **28%** (74 s / 18 s). Authors note true accuracy likely lower (lenient ≥70% threshold metric), and suggest few-shot as next step.
  - Lessons quoted: label descriptions must stay fine-grained ("es lohnt sich nicht, die Genauigkeit der Informationen in den einzelnen Themenblöcken künstlich zu reduzieren"); per-pair zero-shot NLI scales O(labels) and is too slow → directly relevant to 300k × ~200 labels.
  - No code published (no GitHub link in page/report).

## 4. Other products / international prior art

### 4a. ParlaMint / ParlaCAP (CLARIN.SI, JSI) — the closest open methodological template
- `classla/ParlaCAP-Topic-Classifier`: fine-tuned **XLM-R-Parla** (XLM-RoBERTa-large further pretrained on parliamentary proceedings, 0.6B). Labels: **21 CAP major topics + "Other"** (Education, Technology, Health, Environment, Housing, Labor, Defense, Government Operations, Social Welfare, Macroeconomics, Domestic Commerce, Civil Rights, International Affairs, Transportation, Immigration, Law and Crime, Agriculture, Foreign Trade, Culture, Public Lands, Energy). https://huggingface.co/classla/ParlaCAP-Topic-Classifier
- Method: **LLM teacher–student** — GPT-4o zero-shot labels on ParlaCAP-train (29,779 speeches, 29 ParlaMint 4.1 datasets per card; 35,579 speeches / 5.3M words per CLARIN record), then fine-tune encoder. https://www.clarin.si/repository/xmlui/handle/11356/2093 ; paper "Supercharging Agenda Setting Research: The ParlaCAP Dataset of 28 European Parliaments…" https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.16516 (Feb 2026; ParlaCLARIN 2026 https://aclanthology.org/2026.parlaclarin-1.1/)
- Metrics: human-labelled, label-balanced test (~880/lang): **macro-F1 en 0.723, sr 0.710, hr 0.686, bs 0.646**; excluding low-confidence (<0.60 → "Mix", 8.9–11.4% of items) accuracy en 0.761 … bs 0.686. GPT-4o teacher vs humans macro-F1 0.63–0.74. Applied to **>8M speeches, 28 parliaments**.
- Relevance: (1) shows the ceiling for a ~20-class policy-topic task even with a strong teacher is ~0.7 macro-F1 → APS 2-level fine labels will be lower [guess]; (2) teacher–student distillation is the scaling recipe for 300k affairs; (3) "Mix"/abstain at confidence <0.6 is the accepted practice; (4) CAP "Technology" ≈ digitalisation is only a partial match for DVS [guess]. Note ParlaMint has no Swiss parliament [guess: ParlaMint covers EU/EEA + UA/TR etc.; CH not listed in card].

## 6. What a naive hackathon team builds — and where it breaks
Naive build [guess]: prompt Apertus-8B with title(+text) and the ~30 APS main topics, ask for JSON; "ad-hoc topics" = put the user's word in the prompt; demo on 10–20 hand-picked federal affairs; Streamlit UI; "looks right".
Where it breaks (each grounded):
1. **No evaluation / cherry-picked** — the St.Gallen 2023 hack and every commercial tool report no numbers (3b, 4d). Christian's own 2A deck pain: "No gold standard. No metric" (R4 §2.5).
2. **Second level kills flat prompting**: ~200 sub-topics (BFH has 209; APS similar scale [guess]) don't fit cleanly in a prompt per call; 8B models drift outside the vocabulary; ParlaCAP shows even ~21 classes with GPT-4o teacher tops out at ~0.72 macro-F1 (4a).
3. **Zero-shot pairwise scoring doesn't scale**: ZH needed 241 s/affair for 129 labels on CPU; merging label descriptions to go faster dropped accuracy 73%→28% (3a). 300k affairs × hundreds of labels × LLM calls = days of GPU unless designed (two-stage retrieval → rerank, or distillation).
4. **DVS gold is positive-only and small** (~20–30 public, manual, relevance-curated, 3f): naive teams report "precision" against it, which is meaningless; recall@k and blind-annotated precision on a random sample is the honest design.
5. **Federal-only demo**: BFH, DemocraSci, Demokratis(federal Fedlex), ZH all single-level or single-language; cantonal/municipal affairs are short, PDF-heavy, mix question + executive answer (St.Gallen limitation, 3b). Works on Curia Vista ≠ works on 90 parliaments.
6. **Language parity unmeasured**: BFH trains three separate monolingual TF-IDF models (no cross-lingual consistency); Demokratis and ZH are de-only. FR/IT/RM performance is where it silently degrades.
7. **No explanation / no abstention**: deck demands "why" (confidence + passage) and human correction (deck "Ethics: Transparency"); the BFH Space returns bare scores.

## 7. What would make Christian say "clearly #1" [guess, grounded]
- **A real, public, reproducible benchmark** on day 1: (a) federal Curia Vista topics (the BFH label space, obtainable from parlament.ch open data) — beat the **rcds Omikuji baseline on the same split** and show it; (b) APS two-level labels where available; (c) DVS list as recall target + blind-agent-annotated random negatives for precision; (d) Basel-Stadt manual 2019–24 set as cantonal out-of-domain check. Per-level, per-language, per-parliament-level table.
- **Cross-language consistency test**: federal affairs exist in de/fr/it versions [guess: Curia Vista stores titles per language — verify]; same affair, three languages → same topics? Report agreement. Directly answers "multilingual" + "neutrality" criteria.
- **Scale story with numbers**: two-stage design (open multilingual embeddings / Apertus for candidate retrieval → Apertus constrained-choice rerank on top-k, or Apertus-70B teacher → 8B/encoder student, ParlaCAP-style), measured throughput and **cost per 1,000 affairs**, projected wall-clock for 300k on hackathon infra; runs in OPD's FastAPI/Postgres shape and within ~20 GB RAM for the cheap path (R4 §2.5).
- **Ad-hoc topics without retraining, evaluated**: define "Digitalisierung" by a short description + 3–5 seed affairs, return ranked hits with evidence sentence, show recall on DVS list and precision on blind sample; show a second ad-hoc topic (e.g. Covid-19, which the Matoshi study used) to prove generality.
- **Evidence + abstain + correction**: quote the passage, calibrated confidence, "no topic/Mix" below threshold (ParlaCAP <0.6 practice), label must be from the vocabulary (constrained decoding), human override endpoint. Deliver as OPD-ready JSON per affair with `inferred` provenance (his extracted-vs-inferred rule).
- **Openness contrast**: everything others did is closed API (Demokratis/OpenAI), gated (poltextLAB), unmeasured (BFH Space, commercial) or offline (Basel-Stadt) — an Apertus-only, MIT/Apache, measured service fills the exact gap in the deck ("The Gap: thematic structure … not available as a shared, reusable resource").
