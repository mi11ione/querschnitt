### 1b. How an APS page exposes topics (archived process page, capture 2026-04-07: https://web.archive.org/web/20260407004508/https://anneepolitique.swiss/prozesse/60001-organspende-initiative-und-indirekter-gegenvorschlag-brg-20-090?scroll_to=article-61950)
- URL types: `/prozesse/<id>-<slug>` (process = one affair, contains several `<article class="article"><a name="article-<id>">`), `/articles/<id>` (OPD's aps_affair links point here; redirects/anchors into the process [guess]), `/dossiers/<id>-<slug>` (thematic dossiers, e.g. /dossiers/536-dossier-transplantation-…), `?locale=fr` or `/fr/…` for French, `.pdf` export per process.
- Process header: `<div class="topics"><div class="topic-inline colored topic-1-7-2 topic-tag"><h3><a href="/articles?chapters[]=1.7.2.">Gesundheit, Sozialhilfe, Sport</a></h3></div><div class="… topic-tag subtopic"><h4><a href="/articles?keywords[]=1374918">Gesundheitspolitik</a></h4></div></div>` → **exactly one chapter + one main subtopic per process** = clean 2-level label.
- Per article `<dl class="metadata dl-horizontal">`: `Schlagworte` (list of `<span class="label label-keyword topic-1-7-2">Gesundheitspolitik</span>` linking `/articles?keywords[]=<id>`; each keyword carries its chapter as CSS class `topic-X-Y-Z`, so multi-label chapters are recoverable; e.g. Transplantationen (359), Volksabstimmung (688, topic-1-1-3), Referendum (693), Indirekter Gegenvorschlag (704), Vox/Voto (478, topic-1-8-3)), `Datum` ("17. Oktober 2017"), `Prozesstyp` (`process_instruments[]=volksinitiative|motion|parlamentarische_initiative|bundesratsgeschaeft|verwaltungsakt…`), `Geschäftsnr.` (Curia number, e.g. "20.090" → join key to OPD CHE affairs), `Akteure` (persons/parties/cantons/organisations labels), `Quellen` (press refs, BBl PDFs), byline author + "Aktualisiert am 14.06.2024". Article body: German or French prose summary (written by APS editors) with `person-reference`/`article-reference data-term="28798"` spans.
- Article texts are themselves a high-quality summary corpus (de/fr) — but copyrighted [see licence].

### 1c. APS scope, size, languages, licence (FAQ, archived 2026-02-15: https://web.archive.org/web/20260215045055/https://anneepolitique.swiss/pages/additional_information)
- FAQ confirms "**einem der 24 Politikfelder**" (= the 24 chapters above) + a separate full Schlagwort list (finer keywords; count not retrieved) + actor search (persons, parties, associations, cantons, cities, countries) + process types + dossiers + search by Curia Geschäftsnummer.
- Editors write in their mother tongue, **German or French only**; FAQ explicitly apologises to Italian- and Romansh-speakers → no it/rm label names or texts.
- Printed yearbook until 2014; online-only since 2015; yearbook articles **from 2000 onward** are on the platform (older ones being migrated manually; legacy HTML by event-year+theme).
